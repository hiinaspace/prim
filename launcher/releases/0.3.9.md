# Prim 0.3.9

- Add wrist mic and deafen controls during peek: look at either target to toggle.
  Panels follow your wrists, face the headset, and show dwell progress. Deafen
  silences incoming Prim voices/media without muting your microphone.
- Click the desktop scene for mouse yaw while VR tracking is active. Sticks can
  move/turn in Prim during peek after returning to neutral; the game still gets
  those inputs.
- Improve Envision/Monado status-library selection and allow deliberate reveal
  when scene status is unknown. Comfort → Draw Prim above other overlays is a
  compatibility option for unpatched WayVR; it also covers WayVR panels.
- Add opt-in Comfort → Experimental SteamVR peek with stereo OpenVR overlays.
  Tracking-only mode remains available. Linux SteamVR stereo/gesture and Monado
  wrist controls were headset-tested; Windows overlay and other controller
  profiles still need friends testing.
- Keep room, voice and media alive across scene/overlay handoffs. Fix explicit
  Hide being overridden by a latched lift gesture.

Room protocol remains 4. Existing 0.3.6+ peers remain compatible; update to 0.3.9
for these local controls and companion fixes.
