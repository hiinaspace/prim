# Prim 0.3.6

- Enter and leave VR from the Room menu while keeping the room, voice and video running. The launcher now has one Play button, with desktop fallback when VR is unavailable.
- Desktop menus appear in 2D; VR menus draw over scene geometry. Movie controls have their own tab, with clearer microphone and VR controls.
- Anyone in the room can share a local video. Browse, paste or drop a file in Movie, then choose to share it or play it only locally. The room host still controls shared playback; providers control their upload and relay permissions.
- Embedded subtitle selection and direct stereo playback are available in Movie. Spatial movie and voice audio use point-source HRTF rendering, with corrected stereo-channel alignment.
- Play externally hosted livestreams, including RTSP over TCP. Live sources bypass movie pause/seek synchronization and offer a local reconnect button. OBS publishing is configured separately.

Everyone in a room must update to 0.3.6; the participant-sharing protocol is incompatible with 0.3.5.

The Linux single-client headset, menu switching, media audio and livestream checks were user-tested. Multiplayer and Windows/Wine automated checks supplement this; native Windows/SteamVR, WiVRn, Internet relay transitions and longer friend sessions remain testing targets. Invisible tracking alongside another VR game, integrated capture and host migration are not included.
